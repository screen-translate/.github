# Screen Translate

**Your screen. In your language. In one click.**

Screen Translate is an offline screen translator for Mac. Translate text in apps, websites, images, and games — even when you cannot select or copy it. The translation appears over a snapshot of your screen, preserving the original text’s layout and colors.

[Download on the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/screen-translate-fast-easy/id6789973323) · [Website](https://screen-translate.app)

![Screen Translate translating English text into Korean on Mac](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/screen-translate/.github/main/profile/assets/demo-ko.webp)

## What you can do

- **Translate text anywhere on your screen:** understand buttons, settings, and messages in apps; read websites, game dialogue, and quest descriptions; translate screenshots, diagrams, and photos.
- **Read translations in context:** text size and line breaks adapt to the original layout, colors match their surroundings, and the original letters are hidden.
- **Translate offline:** everything is processed on your Mac.
- **Start with a shortcut:** press **⇧⌘2** or use the menu bar. Zoom in with your trackpad and press **Escape** to return to your screen. You can change the shortcut in Settings.
